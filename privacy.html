Privacy at dicotic

Effective date: May 1, 2026

dicotic (the app) does not collect any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

dicotic is designed to run entirely on your device.

We do not track you. We do not build profiles. We do not use analytics that follow you.

dicotic may use anonymous clicks to improve the app. These cannot identify you.

If you sign up for updates, dicotic stores your email address only for that purpose.

If you play audio from other apps or services, those services follow their own privacy policies.

dicotic is built for each one of us humans.

If dicotic ever changes how data is handled, this page and the effective date will be updated.
